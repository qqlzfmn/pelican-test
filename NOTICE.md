# Notice — originality and homage

《鹈鹕测试》 is an original procedural film. It shares a genre with
*67 — A Brief History of Art* (a figure moving through art history) but not its
expression: the subject, road, framing device, narration, jokes, scene
compositions, briefs, music and code are independently created.

Not used, in any form:

- frames, renders, cover art, video or audio from the reference project;
- its narration samples, TTS performances, music or sound design;
- its art briefs, scene compositions, specific gags, year counter, museum label
  cards or venetian-blind transitions.

The film's device is a continuous road and a physical picture frame that the
pelican rides through. Each era is a 1000×1000 painted page on that road.

## Public domain and fictional media

- Referenced historical works (Saharan rock art, Egyptian tomb painting, Han
  stone rubbings, Tang line drawing, Song landscape, *Qingming Shanghe Tu*, Maya
  codices, Mughal miniatures, Bada Shanren, wayang kulit, ukiyo-e, *The Starry
  Night*, *The Scream*, *Nosferatu*) are public domain. No reproductions are
  used; all imagery is procedural.
- The chat client, short-video app, game HUD and press conference are fictional.
  No real logos, icons, mascots, level layouts or notification sounds appear.
- The book 《鹈鹕骑行艺术通史》 and its citations are fictional.
- The score is synthesized from oscillators and noise; no external audio assets.
