# Phase 3 briefs — the remaining seventeen eras

Read `STYLE.md` first. Each section is a self-contained commission. Keep the
scene contract and metadata; replace the placeholder `draw()` and add a
`titleCard(g, { opacity, lift })`. Use `node tools/shot.mjs --t <seconds>` and
inspect the PNG before claiming a pass. Three visual iterations minimum.

Shared facts: scene space is 1000 units tall, ground at y=820, rider at x≈468,
bleed x ∈ [-800, 1600], y ∈ [-150, 1100]. The road helper already scrolls at the
wheel speed. Static art should be cached at 1.2 px/unit. No external images,
audio, logos or copyrighted characters.

---

## s02 — 埃及壁画 / Egyptian Tomb Painting (约前1300; t=11–17)

- **Substrate:** plaster over mudbrick; warm sandstone, hairline cracks, chipped
  edges. Faint red ochre artist's grid and an unfinished foot under the paint.
- **Composition:** three registers. Top: hieroglyph frieze with a cartouche
  containing “鹈鹕测试” plus invented glyphs. Middle: the pelican on the bicycle
  in strict Egyptian profile, both legs and both wheels visible, eye frontal.
  A seated scribe with palette/reed and a small offering table. Bottom: lotus,
  papyrus, a cat, bread loaves.
- **Gag:** on the strong beat the pelican snaps into canonical profile and freezes
  for half a beat; the wheels keep turning. The scribe makes a tally mark.
- **Palette:** plaster #d8c49c, lapis #2b4d7a, turquoise #2f8b86, ochre #b47a2a,
  charcoal #24211c, linen #f1e6c8.
- **Technique:** flat fills inside dark outlines, no shading; broad collar and
  wing patterns; paint loss patches. Quantise only the scribe's tally.
- **Title card:** a carved cartouche panel, blue/gold, Chinese + English + date,
  small wedjat eye. Keep it clear of the rider.

## s03 — 汉画像石拓片 / Han Stone Rubbing (约100; t=17–23)

- **Substrate:** black ink rubbing on fibrous paper; stone chisel grooves,
  creases, white scuffs, torn corners.
- **Composition:** a chariot procession: two horses, a canopy cart, outriders,
  officials with winged caps. The pelican bicycle rides in the queue. A flock of
  birds and a low horizon. Figures in flat silhouette with white interior lines.
- **Gag:** the lead horse looks back; an official's cap flies off; a small seal
  caption reads “异兽” (strange beast).
- **Palette:** black #141311, rubbing greys #d9d2c2/#a89f8d, one faded vermilion
  seal.
- **Technique:** white-line drawing on black, chiselled wobble, no gradients;
  paper grain overlay. Rider painted as white linework, not the standard fill.
- **Title card:** white rubbing text on black, red seal, rough stone edge.

## s04 — 唐代白描 / Tang Line Drawing (约750; t=23–29)

- **Substrate:** warm silk/paper, subtle weave, tea stains.
- **Composition:** pure ink linework, “吴带当风”: the pelican's feathers and long
  scarf stream to the right; bicycle drawn with a few confident lines; bamboo and
  a rock at the edge; a floating apsara ribbon in the sky, no fill.
- **Gag:** the wind lifts the pouch and scarf; the pelican keeps a deadpan face;
  one wheel becomes an ink spiral.
- **Palette:** paper #efe6d3, ink #2b2722, pale sepia #9c7b4f, seal red.
- **Technique:** even, unmodulated line; no shading; line weight changes only at
  turns; a few dry-brush breaks. Title card as a vertical colophon.
- **Title card:** thin vertical ink inscription + small red seal.

## s06 — 清明上河图 / Along the River During Qingming (约1100; t=35–41)

- **Substrate:** silk with ink and light mineral colour; aged.
- **Composition:** the Rainbow Bridge arches over a river; boats, shops, willows,
  porters, children, a donkey. The pelican crosses the bridge; the crowd turns.
  Foreground water reflections; distant rooftops.
- **Gag:** bridge traffic jam; a porter drops a basket; a boatman ducks under the
  bridge; a shop sign reads “虹桥”.
- **Palette:** silk #e8ddc4, ink #31332a, willow green #5e6b52, vermilion #a84b32,
  roof grey #7d7a6d.
- **Technique:** fine ink outlines, flat pale washes, tiny figures in groups;
  no perspective vanishing point.
- **Title card:** a small shop-sign/seal panel, vertical Chinese, ink.

## s07 — 玛雅抄本 / Maya Codex (约1200; t=41–47)

- **Substrate:** bark paper, red/black pigment, one folded page with a crease.
- **Composition:** a page with glyph columns and a bar-and-dot counter in the
  margin; pelican in Maya profile with a long beak and a glyph-like bicycle; a
  scribe/deity with an earspool; a rabbit scribe. Red frame lines.
- **Gag:** the bar-and-dot counter records pedal revolutions and rolls over on a
  strong beat; the rabbit updates it.
- **Palette:** bark #c8a06a, Maya red #a8492f, black #2a1710, cream #f0d7a2,
  turquoise #2f7d76.
- **Technique:** flat codical linework, block glyphs, no shading; quantise the
  counter to 10 Hz.
- **Title card:** a carved glyph block with bar/dot numerals, red/black.

## s08 — 莫卧儿细密画 / Mughal Miniature (约1600; t=47–53)

- **Substrate:** burnished paper, gold leaf, fine brush.
- **Composition:** a charbagh garden with a marble terrace, cypress, fountain,
  and a gold floral border. Four courtiers watch from different viewpoints at
  once (a deliberate flat-perspective joke); the pelican rides a path.
- **Gag:** all four heads turn at slightly different times/angles; one courtier
  offers the pelican a flower; a servant freezes mid-pour.
- **Palette:** ivory #efe0c2, emerald #2f6b4e, gold #b78a33, rose #a34b3f,
  lapis #2b4f7d.
- **Technique:** fine outlines, flat jewel colours, gold details, no cast shadow;
  decorative border.
- **Title card:** gold-framed panel, tiny floral ornament.

## s09 — 八大山人写意 / Bada Shanren (约1690; t=53–59)

- **Substrate:** off-white paper, huge negative space.
- **Composition:** pelican/bicycle in very few wet ink strokes; a rock, a lone
  fish, a dry branch. The pelican gives the viewer the signature rolled side-eye.
  A red seal and vertical inscription.
- **Gag:** the fish returns the side-eye; the pelican blinks first.
- **Palette:** paper #eee9de, ink #23211d, grey wash #857e6e, seal red.
- **Technique:** loaded brush, dry breaks, no fill; less is more. Do not add
  texture for its own sake.
- **Title card:** vertical calligraphy, seal, minimal.

## s10 — 爪哇皮影 / Wayang Kulit (约1800; t=59–65)

- **Substrate:** cotton screen lit by an oil lamp; deep indigo surround.
- **Composition:** carved leather pelican and bicycle puppets with visible rods
  and pin holes; ornate border; a puppet cow and a dalang's hand at the bottom.
  Warm amber light, hard shadow.
- **Gag:** the wheels advance in 8–12 Hz steps; the shadow lags one step behind;
  a rod nudges the pelican and it keeps riding.
- **Palette:** indigo #191a26, amber #d99a3e, gold #e0b05a, lamp white.
- **Technique:** black silhouette with amber cutouts, thin gold outlines, visible
  rods; quantise puppet motion to 10 Hz; camera smooth.
- **Title card:** carved shadow-play frame, gold on indigo.

## s11 — 浮世绘 / Ukiyo-e (1830s; t=65–71)

- **Substrate:** woodblock print on cream paper; visible registration.
- **Composition:** Tokaido road, milestone, pine, tea house, travelers, Mt Fuji;
  the pelican rides past a signpost. Prussian blue sky with bokashi gradient.
- **Gag:** the signpost reads “东海道 五十三次 · 鹈鹕”; a traveler bows to the
  wrong person; a teahouse girl points.
- **Palette:** Prussian #254b6b, cream #e7ddc7, vermilion #b4452c, pine #3f5b47,
  Fuji blue #6f8fa3.
- **Technique:** flat colour, clean keyblock outline, gradient sky, no shading.
- **Title card:** vertical cartouche + red seal.

## s12 — 星月夜 / The Starry Night (1889; t=71–77)

- **Substrate:** canvas with thick impasto; visible directional strokes.
- **Composition:** swirling night sky, crescent moon, stars, cypress on the left,
  village below, pelican/bike on the road. The wheel rotation feeds the swirl.
- **Gag:** a star becomes a spinning wheel; the swirl follows the pedals.
- **Palette:** deep blue #17294f, cobalt #1f3a66, yellow #e8c14f, pale #f2d46a,
  cypress #1b2b22.
- **Technique:** short thick curved strokes in flow fields; layered paint; no
  smooth gradients. Cache the base sky; animate a slow swirl offset.
- **Title card:** yellow brush lettering on deep blue.

## s13 — 呐喊 / The Scream (1893; t=77–83)

- **Substrate:** painted board, wavy impasto.
- **Composition:** diagonal bridge railing, orange/red sky, blue-black fjord,
  two distant walkers, the screaming figure in the foreground. The pelican rides
  past; the screamer becomes a startled onlooker.
- **Gag:** the scream is because the pelican rings its bell; the pelican is
  unbothered; the wavy sky pulses on the beat.
- **Palette:** orange #d4692e, blood #b64925, blue-black #2c2f3a, ochre #f0b35a.
- **Technique:** long wavy contours, flat bands, no realism; animate the waves.
- **Title card:** wavy orange/black panel.

## s14 — 诺斯费拉图 / Nosferatu (1922; t=83–89)

- **Substrate:** black-and-white silent film, heavy grain, vignette, scratches.
- **Composition:** a staircase wall and arched window; the pelican/bicycle exists
  only as a hard shadow climbing the wall, growing. An original vampire
  silhouette in the doorway (no actor likeness). Intertitle.
- **Gag:** the shadow pedals up the wall; at the end its outline charges with
  green phosphor (the 14→15 hand-off).
- **Palette:** black #0d0b09, bone #d8cdb8, grey #6f6555, one green #59ffb0 at the
  end.
- **Technique:** high-contrast shadow shapes, film gate weave, scratch lines;
  quantise the shadow to 12 Hz. Near local 5.6–6.0 tint the shadow green.
- **Title card:** silent-film intertitle with ornate border.

## s15 — 示波器矢量图 / Oscilloscope (1950s; t=89–95)

- **Substrate:** round green CRT, black surround, scanlines, phosphor glow.
- **Composition:** vector pelican/bicycle drawn in glowing green lines on a graticule
  grid; persistence trails; a hand turning a knob at the edge; Lissajous figure.
- **Gag:** the hand turns the wrong knob; the pelican twists into a Lissajous
  knot for one beat, then snaps back. Pitch bends with it (audio later).
- **Palette:** black #020604, phosphor #42f59e, bright #7dffbf, dim #0b2a18.
- **Technique:** additive glowing strokes, double-exposure trails, scan jitter;
  use `lighter` compositing. The scene should begin already green so the 14→15
  hand-off is seamless.
- **Title card:** instrument label / CRT overlay, green.

## s16 — 8-bit 像素游戏 / 8-Bit Arcade (1980s; t=95–101)

- **Substrate:** CRT pixels, limited palette, chunky 1–3 px grid.
- **Composition:** side-scrolling level: parallax hills, brick/ground tiles, a
  pit, floating coins, an original “1UP” banner, score HUD. Pixel pelican/bike
  jumps the pit on the beat.
- **Gag:** coin + 1UP on the strong beat; the pelican's legs keep pedalling in
  mid-air; a tiny “BUG?” flickers.
- **Palette:** navy #101a38, magenta #e7438d, cyan #4de1ff, yellow #f7d94c,
  white #f2f4f8, black.
- **Technique:** render at integer pixels; quantise gameplay to 8–10 Hz; camera
  and HUD can stay smooth. No real game characters or logos.
- **Title card:** pixel-font block/level card.

## s17 — 早期聊天窗口 / Early Chat Client (2000s; t=101–107)

- **Substrate:** fictional desktop, blue window chrome, gradient title bar.
- **Composition:** a chat window with contact list and a received GIF of the
  pelican riding; 256-colour dithering, low frame rate. Top says “对方正在输入…”.
  No real app names/icons.
- **Gag:** the window shakes on the strong beat; the GIF loops at 8–10 Hz; a
  message arrives “这个 GIF 我存了”.
- **Palette:** silver-blue #dfe7ee, steel #4f7ea8, deep blue #2f76b5, white,
  black text.
- **Technique:** chunky UI, gradients, pixel/dither GIF area; quantise the GIF
  and typing dots to 10 Hz. The window and camera stay smooth.
- **Title card:** fictional app title-bar card.

## s18 — 蒸汽波 / Vaporwave (2010s; t=107–113)

- **Substrate:** VHS/CRT, magenta-cyan gradient, scanlines, chromatic fringe.
- **Composition:** perspective neon grid to a setting sun, low-poly palms, a
  classical marble bust watching from the side, the pelican/bike in low-poly
  silhouette. Floating original UI fragments (no real brands).
- **Gag:** the marble bust slowly turns its head on a strong beat; the VHS
  tracking glitches; a “PLEASE REWIND” style original sticker.
- **Palette:** #2b1b4d, #3b2270, #ff6fb5, #61e8e1, cream #f7d8ef.
- **Technique:** flat gradients, grid lines, scanlines, RGB offset; no realistic
  rendering. Music will be the slowed tape (phase 4).
- **Title card:** chrome/pink VHS label.

## s20 — 代码美学 / Code Aesthetic (2020s; t=119–125)

- **Substrate:** dark editor, syntax highlighting, line numbers, cursor.
- **Composition:** a clean editor with SVG/JS code typing line by line on the
  left and a live preview of the pelican/bike being stroked in on the right. The
  final frame is the most refined drawing in the film.
- **Gag:** a comment “// TODO: 不要被牛群发现” types itself; a benign warning
  “unused variable: dignity”; the cursor blinks on the beat.
- **Palette:** #0d1117, #58a6ff, #7ee787, #ffa657, #c9d1d9.
- **Technique:** monospace text, 1 px UI, SVG-like vector preview, typewriter
  reveal tied to bars; cache the editor chrome. Keep it genuinely legible.
- **Title card:** editor tab/file card with the scene name.
